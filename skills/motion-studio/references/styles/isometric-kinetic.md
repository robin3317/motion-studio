# Style: Isometric Kinetic
**Feel:** smart, crisp, editorial-tech. A cut-paper isometric world with kinetic headlines on top. Explains things as it sells them.
**Template:** `python scripts/new_project.py ... --template isometric-kinetic`. It creates a 6-scene composition plus `lib/iso-kit.js`, and works in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5. Edit the `C` (copy) and `BRAND` (tokens) objects and keep the rhythm.

- **Background rhythm (the signature):** most scenes on a **dark** brand-tinted charcoal. Every 3–4 scenes, one scene on **paper** (warm off-white). The one or two biggest moments (key number, hero claim) go on a **solid** brand colour. Flat fills only: no gradients, glows or photo backgrounds behind type.
- **Colour from the brand:** take the brand's darkest tone and push it to near-black for *dark* (e.g. #08251a for a green brand). *Solid* = the brand's loudest colour. *Accent on dark* = a lighter tint of the brand colour that passes 4.5:1 on dark. *Accent on light* = a deeper tint that passes on paper. Keep all of them in the template's single `BRAND` object. The page CSS and the isometric objects both read from it.
- **Type:** geometric sans for headlines (default **Outfit** 400/700, OFL via `@fontsource/outfit`) plus a mono for eyebrows, HUD, tags and numbers (default **JetBrains Mono** 500/700). Headlines are sentence case or lowercase, regular weight, left-aligned, 1–3 lines. **Exactly one accent word** per headline, bold, in the accent colour. A brand font can replace Outfit if it's geometric and licensed.
- **Eyebrow:** `—— MONO CAPS · CONTEXT` above the headline at about 45–60% opacity.
- **HUD (optional, good for explainers and series):** top-left `● BRAND · CAMPAIGN`; top-right `SCENE 03 / NAME` + timecode, driven by the clock (`IsoKit.hud`). Recolour it per scene background (it must pass contrast). Leave it off for short social ads under 15 s.
- **Isometric objects:** 2:1 projection, flat 3-tone faces (top lightest, left mid, right darkest), 3–4 px dark outlines. Objects are neutral off-white, and the **hero object takes the brand colour** with the logo or a label on its top face. Every object stands on a plate or tile. Dashed outlines show "empty → filled". Use `IsoKit.box/plate/tile/ring`.
- **Motion:** fast in → hold → cut. Headline lines rise out of a mask (`expo.out`, 0.5–0.6 s, 0.1 s stagger), **each line on the word that says it** (VO word times), or on music beats when there's no VO. Objects drop in from above with a small overshoot (`back.out(1.6)`, 0.4–0.6 s, staggered 0.06–0.1 s). Ambient motion during holds: slow orbit, float, travelling pills. Counters ease out.
- **Transitions:** **hard cuts** on beats, every 3–4 s, with no wipes or dissolves. The rhythm comes from the background changing colour.
- **Captions (when there's a voice):** a dark pill, bottom-centre, 2–4 words. The spoken word sits on a brand-solid block with dark text (`caption-highlight` card, restyled).
- **Shots:** `iso-statement`, `iso-orbit-hero`, `iso-platform-row`, `exploded-stack`, `stat-counter` (on solid, with a cube grid), `terminal-window`, `timeline-axis`, `countdown-flip` (iso-kit `flip()`), `cta-end-card`. Never the same card twice in a row.
- **Music:** light electronic / lo-fi / minimal tech pulse, 100–124 BPM, with clear beats to cut on. **SFX:** soft clicks on line reveals, wooden or plastic "tok" on object drops, short whoosh on orbit start, one low hit on the solid-colour scene.
- **Fits:** SaaS/tech company promos, digital products and apps, courses and dev content, explainers, B2B, event and release announcements with facts. **Less suited** to luxury, fashion, food or emotional brand films: use Minimal Luxury, Editorial or Cinematic 3D for those.
- **Avoid:** more than one accent word; gradients or glow on objects; perspective 3D mixed into the same scene (keep iso flat); text on object faces smaller than 28 px at render size; too many objects (one idea = one object group); cutting before a headline line has held at least 1 s.

**Background:** the frame study behind this style is in the GitHub repo's docs folder (`10-isometric-kinetic-analysis`), not needed at run time. Craft rules that apply to every style are in `../craft.md`.
