# Style: Cinematic 3D
**Feel:** epic, dimensional, premium, trailer-like.
- **Background:** dark gradients, volumetric haze, subtle particles (seeded). Letterbox bars optional for 16:9.
- **Colour:** low-key lighting with brand-coloured rim or key lights. Metallic and glassy materials.
- **Type:** wide or condensed display (Space Grotesk, Bebas Neue, Cinzel-like serifs), spaced Latin caps, small and elegant. Text appears with light sweeps or fades from blur.
- **Motion:** slow, weighty camera moves (orbits 20–40°, dolly-ins), 2–4 s per move. Ease `easeInOutCubic`. Hits land on music downbeats.
- **Transitions:** light flares, dip to black, camera flies through the logo or object.
- **Shots:** `3d-logo-reveal`, `product-hero-orbit`, `parallax-push`, `cta-end-card`. Follow `three-d.md`.
- **Music:** cinematic / trailer hybrid, 80–110 BPM with big hits. **SFX:** deep booms, sub drops, metallic swooshes, risers.
- **Avoid:** cartoonish colours, fast snappy UI motion, overcrowded frames.
