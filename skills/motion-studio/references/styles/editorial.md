# Style: Editorial
**Feel:** magazine spread, artful, confident, grid-based.
- **Background:** paper tones (off-white, newsprint) or bold flat colour. Strong grid with visible margins and rules.
- **Colour:** restrained, with 1 bold accent. Black type dominates.
- **Type:** high-contrast serif headlines (Fraunces / Playfair Display) + a grotesk (Inter / Space Grotesk). Big numerals, pull quotes, index labels ("01 / 03"). Mixed sizes for hierarchy.
- **Motion:** confident and graphic. Masked line reveals, image crops that expand, columns sliding on the grid. Ease `power3.inOut`. Entrances 0.4–0.7 s.
- **Transitions:** page-turn slides, grid column wipes, image-to-image crop morphs.
- **Graphics:** photo crops, duotones, thin rules, captions, numbered sections.
- **Shots:** `kinetic-title` (serif), `parallax-push` (photo layers), `logo-wall`, `shape-wipe`, `cta-end-card`.
- **Music:** indie / lo-fi / jazzy / minimal beats, 85–115 BPM. **SFX:** paper slides, camera shutters, soft thuds.
- **Avoid:** neon glows, 3D bevels, cluttered centred layouts.
