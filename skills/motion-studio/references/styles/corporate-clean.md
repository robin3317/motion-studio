# Style: Corporate Clean
**Feel:** clear, trustworthy, calm confidence.
- **Background:** light (white / off-white #F7F8FA) or a deep brand navy. Lots of whitespace.
- **Colour:** one brand accent + neutrals (greys). Accent for key words, lines and icons only.
- **Type:** Inter / Manrope / Poppins. Headline 600–700 weight, support 400–500. Sentence case.
- **Motion:** smooth and purposeful. Ease `power2.out`/`easeOutCubic`. Entrances 0.4–0.6 s, slide 20–40 px + fade. Gentle stagger 0.06–0.1 s. Holds ≥ 1.5 s.
- **Transitions:** clean wipes, slide pushes, soft cross-dissolves (≤ 0.4 s).
- **Graphics:** thin lines, rounded cards (radius 16–24), simple line icons, subtle shadows, real screenshots in frames.
- **Shots:** `feature-grid`, `stat-counter`, `logo-wall`, `device-mockup-screens`, `cta-end-card`.
- **Music:** modern corporate / light electronic, 100–125 BPM. **SFX:** soft ticks, light whooshes, subtle pops.
- **Avoid:** heavy glows, aggressive shakes, more than 2 font families.
