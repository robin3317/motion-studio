# Style: Minimal Luxury
**Feel:** quiet, elegant, expensive, unhurried.
- **Background:** cream, warm grey, black or deep green. Huge negative space. Fine film grain (subtle).
- **Colour:** muted palette, one metallic or brand accent used rarely.
- **Type:** Playfair Display / Cormorant / DM Serif Display headlines + a light sans (Manrope 300–400). Generous letter-spacing on small Latin caps. Small sizes, centred or asymmetric.
- **Motion:** slow and soft. Fades 0.8–1.2 s, gentle drifts (10–20 px), slow scale 1.0 → 1.04. Ease `sine.inOut`. Long holds 2–3 s.
- **Transitions:** slow cross-dissolves, soft masks, fade through the background colour.
- **Shots:** `product-hero-orbit` (slow), `parallax-push`, `logo-assemble` (delicate), `cta-end-card`.
- **Music:** ambient piano / minimal strings / downtempo, 60–95 BPM. **SFX:** very sparse: soft shimmer, paper or fabric textures.
- **Avoid:** bright saturated colours, bounces, many words, busy layouts.
