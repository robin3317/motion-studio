# Style: Bold Kinetic
**Feel:** loud, energetic, type-driven, scroll-stopping.
- **Background:** solid high-contrast fields (black, white or the brand colour) that flip between beats.
- **Colour:** 2–3 colours max, high saturation. Colour blocks swap on the beat.
- **Type:** Anton / Bebas Neue / Space Grotesk Bold (Latin). Huge headlines (150–260 px), tight leading 0.9–1.0, uppercase OK for Latin. For complex scripts, use bold weights of the script's font and keep the line height (`typography-i18n.md`).
- **Motion:** fast and snappy. Ease `expo.out`/`back.out(1.4)`. Entrances 0.2–0.35 s. Word-by-word reveals, scale pops (0.8 → 1.0), masks, slams. Cut every 0.6–1.5 s on the beat.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts on the beat, colour-block wipes, `shape-wipe`.
- **Shots:** `kinetic-title`, `shape-wipe`, `stat-counter`, `countdown-flip`, `cta-end-card`.
- **Music:** punchy, beat-forward, 120–140 BPM. **SFX:** impacts, swishes, risers before reveals.
- **Avoid:** small text, long holds, soft pastel palettes, readability shortcuts (still respect reading time).
