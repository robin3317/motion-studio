# Style: Playful Pop
**Feel:** fun, friendly, bouncy, colourful.
- **Background:** bright solid colours, simple patterns, blobs and confetti shapes.
- **Colour:** 3–5 vivid colours, the brand colour leading. Thick outlines optional.
- **Type:** Poppins Bold / Baloo 2 / rounded sans. Large, friendly, mixed weights, playful tilt (±3°).
- **Motion:** bouncy. Ease `back.out(1.7)`/`elastic.out(1, 0.5)` for pops. Squash and stretch on shapes. Entrances 0.3–0.5 s, stagger 0.05–0.08 s.
- **Transitions:** blob/circle wipes, pop-in shapes, slide with overshoot.
- **Graphics:** stickers, emoji-like icons (original, not branded), hand-drawn underlines, wobbly shapes.
- **Shots:** `feature-grid` (bouncy), `product-float-callouts`, `kinetic-title`, `shape-wipe`, `cta-end-card`.
- **Music:** upbeat pop / ukulele / funky, 110–130 BPM. **SFX:** pops, boings, bubbly clicks, cartoon whooshes.
- **Avoid:** dark moody palettes, slow fades, corporate stiffness.
