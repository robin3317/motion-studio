# iso-platform-row
**Use for:** 2–4 parallel things: products in a suite, plans, steps, platforms ("web · mobile · desktop"), chapters of a course.  **Length:** 3–4.5 s
**Needs:** 2–4 item names (1–3 words), optionally an icon-like object per item (cube, server, laptop, document).
**Beats**
- 0.0–0.6: plates drop in left → right (16:9) or top → bottom (9:16), staggered 0.12 s
- 0.4–1.2: one object lands on each plate (template: plates drop with their objects). Variant: a dashed outline first, then it fills with colour ("empty → filled" = "we provide this")
- 0.6–1.4: a mono caption under each plate rises in
- 1.4: the item that matters most for the brand gets the brand colour + a small bounce; the others stay neutral
- Hold ≥ 1 s, then hard cut
**Build:** template scene S3 (paper background works best here). `IsoKit.plate`, `box({dash:true})` then a second `box` at the same spot that fades in, `tile` for numbered chapter tiles.
**SFX:** three soft ticks for the plates, one brighter hit on the highlighted item.
**Avoid:** more than 4 items (split into two scenes); equal emphasis on all items; captions on the plates' faces smaller than 28 px.
**Registry starting points:** none. Build from the template.
