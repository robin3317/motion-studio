# cta-end-card
**Use for:** every ending: logo + call to action.  **Length:** 2.5–4 s (CTA and URL on screen ≥ 2 s; HyperFrames flags shorter closing lines)
**Needs:** logo, CTA text (verb-first: "Book a demo", "Enroll now"), URL/handle, optional offer/date.
**Beats**
- 0.0–0.5: background settles (brand colour or blurred last shot); logo lands (from `logo-assemble` or a scale-in)
- 0.4–0.9: CTA appears as a button-like pill or bold line; URL below
- 1.0+: subtle loop motion (pulse on the CTA pill, slow background drift) until the end
- The music resolves or fades on the last 0.5–1 s
**Ease:** `power3.out`.  **SFX:** final hit on the logo, soft click/pop on the CTA.
**Avoid:** more than one CTA; small URLs; the CTA appearing in the last 1 s only.
**Registry starting points:** cta-lockup, cta-close, logo-brand-close, store-badge-lockup. Check availability with the `/hyperframes-registry` skill (the catalogue changes). Restyle the block to the preset; don't ship it unchanged.
