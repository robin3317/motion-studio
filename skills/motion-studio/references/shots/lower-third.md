# lower-third
**Use for:** speaker name and title, location tags on footage.  **Length:** 3–4 s
**Needs:** name + role (verified spelling).
**Beats**
- 0.0–0.4: accent bar wipes in from the side
- 0.15–0.55: name slides in from behind the bar mask; role follows 0.1 s later (smaller, lighter)
- Hold 2–3 s
- Exit 0.3 s: reverse
**Placement:** lower left (LTR) or lower right (RTL), inside the safe zone, not over the face. 9:16: above the caption zone.
**Ease:** `power3.out` / `power3.in`.  **SFX:** none or a very soft swish (speech has priority).
**Registry starting points:** lt-clean-bar, lt-mask-reveal, lt-kicker-name, lt-side-rule. Check availability with the `/hyperframes-registry` skill (the catalogue changes). Restyle the block to the preset; don't ship it unchanged.
