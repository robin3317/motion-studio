# product-hero-orbit
**Use for:** the product reveal / hero moment.  **Length:** 2.5–5 s
**Needs:** a GLB model **or** a clean cut-out image (2.5D path). See `three-d.md`.
**Beats (3D model)**
- 0.0–0.6: product rises or fades in from blur on a soft shadow plane
- 0.0–end: slow orbit 20–40° (or a turntable rotation), slight dolly-in 10%
- 1.5: light sweep across the surface highlights the material
**Beats (2.5D image)**
- Cut-out on a plane with a 5–10° tilt, contact shadow, background layers moving at different speeds (parallax), subtle scale 1.0 → 1.06
**Ease:** `easeInOutCubic`.  **SFX:** whoosh into the reveal, soft hit on the hero position.
**Avoid:** fake 3D of a real product; reflections that hide the product's true colour.
**Registry starting points:** product-turntable, parallax-zoom, gloss-sweep. Check availability with the `/hyperframes-registry` skill (the catalogue changes). Restyle the block to the preset; don't ship it unchanged.
