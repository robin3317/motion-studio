# countdown-flip
**Use for:** countdown promos ("3 days left"), launch timers, dates.  **Length:** 2–4 s
**Needs:** the number as a **variable** (e.g. `daysLeft`) so one composition renders many versions; the unit word in the right language and plural form.
**Beats**
- 0.0–0.5: previous number flips or slides away, the new number slams in big (center)
- 0.5–1.0: unit word ("days left") and the date line appear
- Hold ≥ 1.5 s. Optional pulse on the beat
**Ease:** `back.out(1.6)` slam, `power2.in` exit.  **SFX:** mechanical flip/tick + impact on the new number.
**Variants:** split-flap board, digital LED digits (Tech Neon), giant outline numerals (Editorial).
**Avoid:** wrong singular/plural forms; missing time zone when the time is shown.
**Registry starting points:** split-flap-board, number-wheel, slot-machine-roll. Check availability with the `/hyperframes-registry` skill (the catalogue changes). Restyle the block to the preset; don't ship it unchanged.
