# kinetic-title
**Use for:** hooks, statements, manifesto lines, transitions between sections.  **Length:** 1.5–4 s
**Needs:** 1–2 short lines (≤ 7 words each).
**Beats**
- 0.0–0.5: words reveal one by one (mask slide-up, scale pop or slam), stagger 0.08–0.15 s
- Mid: hold at full readability for at least the reading time (0.4 s/word + 0.5 s)
- Key word: colour or underline accent 0.2 s after it lands
- Exit 0.2–0.3 s: slide out, or hand off to the next shot via `shape-wipe`
**Ease:** `expo.out` (bold) / `power2.out` (clean).  **SFX:** one swish per line (not per word) or a single impact on the key word.
**i18n:** reveal by **word** for complex scripts. RTL reveals right to left.
**Variants:** stacked big/small hierarchy; line-by-line replace in the same spot; text over moving image with a scrim.
**Registry starting points:** headline-slam, kinetic-center-build, per-word-rise, line-by-line-slide, kinetic-type-swap. Check availability with the `/hyperframes-registry` skill (the catalogue changes). Restyle the block to the preset; don't ship it unchanged.
