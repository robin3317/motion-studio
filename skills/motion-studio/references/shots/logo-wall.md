# logo-wall
**Use for:** clients, partners, "as featured in" (**only if publicly listed** by the company).  **Length:** 2.5–4 s
**Needs:** logos (monochrome-friendly SVG/PNG), a heading ("Trusted by…").
**Beats**
- 0.0–0.4: heading in
- 0.3–1.5: logos appear in a grid, staggered 0.05–0.08 s (fade + 10 px rise), all converted to one tone (white or grey) for harmony
- Optional: a slow horizontal marquee for more than 8 logos
**Ease:** `power2.out`.  **SFX:** a single soft swell, or very subtle ticks grouped.
**Avoid:** implying endorsement; mixing colourful logos at random sizes (normalize their visual weight).
**Registry starting points:** logo-wall, trust-strip. Check availability with the `/hyperframes-registry` skill (the catalogue changes). Restyle the block to the preset; don't ship it unchanged.
