# Playbook: Brand promo (FEELINGS)

**Purpose:** the viewer should *feel* what the brand stands for. Mood first, facts optional.
**Use when:** brand story, brand identity reel, manifesto, logo reveal, values video.

## Inputs
Brand name, logo, palette, tone words (3–5, e.g. "bold, warm, rebellious"), values or a manifesto line, tagline. Helpful: brand imagery, fonts, the audience.

## Defaults
- Style: chosen by the tone words. Bold → **Bold Kinetic**; calm/premium → **Minimal Luxury**; artsy/cultural → **Editorial**; futuristic → **Tech Neon**; fun → **Playful Pop**; epic → **Cinematic 3D**.
- Music carries the emotion. Pick it **before** finalizing the pacing; cut on the phrasing.
- VO: optional. A manifesto-style VO works well at 30–60 s.

## Structure (manifesto arc)
| Part | ~Share | Shot ideas |
|---|---|---|
| Tension / question | 15% | `kinetic-title`, `parallax-push` |
| Belief statements (2–4 lines) | 45% | `kinetic-title` variants, `shape-wipe` transitions, brand imagery |
| Turn: "That's why we…" | 15% | `parallax-push`, `shape-wipe` |
| Logo reveal + tagline | 25% | `3d-logo-reveal` or `logo-assemble` → `cta-end-card` (tagline, optional URL) |

## Rules
- Short, rhythmic lines (3–6 words). Let them breathe. Fewer words than company promos.
- Colour and type do the talking: commit to the palette and use motion consistently.
- Facts are optional. If used, they still come from `facts.json`.

## Pitfalls
Generic inspirational clichés ("We believe in excellence"). Use the brand's own words and values. Over-busy motion that fights the music.
